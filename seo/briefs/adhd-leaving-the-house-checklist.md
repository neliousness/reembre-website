# Brief: adhd leaving the house checklist
Date: 2026-10-27   Mode: new

## Search intent
Adults with ADHD (or who suspect it) who keep walking out without keys, meds, or the one extra
thing that day, looking for a checklist they can use today. Some searchers are parents of kids
with ADHD; the SERP currently serves only them. Google rewards a usable list near the top plus
short advice on making it stick.

## SERP snapshot
- Twinkl (several regional domains): editable kids' checklist sorted into self-care, take to
  school, things to remember. Account needed, written for children.
- checklist.com PDF, Scribd upload, school and clinic PDFs: generic or child-focused.
- Psych Central (Ash Fisher, reviewed 2022): triple check of wallet, phone, keys, ID, pass; a
  departure list on the doorknob. Advice, no copyable list.
- ADDitude expert hacks (updated 2023): Sandy Maynard's launchpad basket by the front door, lay
  out critical items the night before. Tips, no list.
- CHADD Attention Magazine (Dec 2025): time-based vs event-based prospective memory, recommends
  event cues and location-based reminders. Explains, no list.
- Nobody offers an adult, copyable checklist that separates what to carry from what to check,
  or says which part a phone can and cannot cover.

## Information gain (required)
- A copyable adult checklist in three short layers (every trip, today only, house checks),
  built on the launchpad, triple-check and night-before habits the sources above recommend,
  with each habit cited.
- First-hand split from building a leaving-reminder app: a prompt that fires after you have
  moved some real distance is good for "go back for it" items and wrong for door, stove and
  iron checks, which have to stay a doorway habit. No competitor says this.
- The reload step most launchpad advice skips: an arrival checklist that puts things back on
  the launchpad when you get home.
- Per-place lists (home, office, gym) instead of one long list, using CHADD's event-based cue
  framing.
