# Brief: apps for remembering things before you leave
Date: 2026-10-20   Mode: refresh (absorbs the backlog idea "before you go checklists alternative")

## Why a refresh and not a new post
The backlog idea `leaving-checklist-apps-compared` had a note to check overlap with this post.
SERP overlap test, 2026-10-20: "apps for remembering things before you leave the house",
"before you go checklists app alternative", "location based checklist app iphone leave home"
and "app that alerts you when you leave without your items" all return the same App Store
listings (Before You Go, Paranoid List, Before Leaving / ReadyKin, Checking Rituals). Same
intent, so one page. A second URL would also split signals while Google is still not crawling
most of the blog (see learnings 2026-10-20). This post already shows impressions at position 3.

## Search intent
People who keep leaving things behind and want an app that catches it on the way out. Commercial
investigation: they want a shortlist, what each app actually does, and which one fits their phone.

## SERP snapshot
- apps.apple.com: Before You Go, Paranoid List, ReadyKin (formerly Before Leaving), Checking
  Rituals, CheckYourList, Take It, Home Key Reminder. Developer marketing only.
- alternativeto.net: generic checklist apps (Trello, Todoist), not leaving checklists.
- Product Hunt / hunted.space: LeftBehind (Android, Wi-Fi and motion), ReadyGo, Reembr.
- ioshacker.com / cultofmac.com: 2019 write-ups of Don't Leave It! (now gone from the US store).
- No independent page compares trigger type, platform, price and privacy label side by side.

## Information gain (required)
- One dated, source-linked table (checked 2026-10-20) of what starts each checklist (GPS leave,
  Wi-Fi and motion, manual), platforms, listed price, and the developer's privacy label.
- Facts nobody else states: Before Leaving is now ReadyKin and its label lists identifiers used
  for tracking and advertising; almost every leaving-checklist app is iPhone-only; App Store
  privacy labels are self-reported.
- First-hand explanation, from building a geofence app, of why a leave trigger waits for real
  distance and what that means for each trigger type.
- Correction: "Where'd I Put It" could not be found on the App Store or anywhere else on
  2026-10-20, so it is removed rather than described from memory.
