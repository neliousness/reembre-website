Account Deletion

You can delete your Reembr account and its data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

In the app

Open Reembr → Account → Delete account. This is instant and removes:

  • Your login and profile details
  • Checklists saved to your account
  • Your plan status

Places you've saved live only on your phone and are never uploaded, so deleting your account doesn't touch them. Uninstalling the app removes those.

Without the app

No longer have Reembr installed? Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you, typically within a few days.
