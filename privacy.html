Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026 · Applies to the Reembr website and app.

Summary

Reembr is built so that where your places are, and the checks that notice when you leave, stay on your phone. The map imagery and place search inside the app come from OpenStreetMap, so those two features do talk to a third party (details below). The app also uses analytics and crash-reporting tools to see how it’s used and catch bugs (details below too). This website is a marketing site: it collects only what you choose to send us, a waitlist email or a support message, plus basic, privacy-respecting usage analytics.

The app: data on your device

The app stores your saved places and their names, where they are, their reminder areas, whether they’re switched on, availability schedules, linked checklists, reminder preferences, and app settings. All of it stays on your device.

The app: data synced with your account

If you sign in, we may store a sign-in ID, your email, display name, profile photo, sign-in methods, plan status, checklist templates, checklist items, and safe app settings. Where your places are, where you’ve been, and your comings and goings never sync.

The app: location data

In this release we do not store where your places are, their reminder areas, your location readings, or your comings and goings on our servers. Location permission is used on your device to help decide when to show a reminder.

The app: analytics & crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to see which features get used and to catch crashes, and Microsoft Clarity to record app sessions (what you tap, how you move through screens) so we can spot confusing flows and bugs we’d otherwise never see. These tools collect standard technical data (device type, app version, screen views, taps, and crash details) along with a device identifier, but not your saved places, your location, or anything from your account. Firebase Analytics may also use your device’s advertising ID for this purpose; we don’t use it for ads, since this app doesn’t show any.

The app: maps & place search

Two parts of the app do reach a third party, and neither one is us. Map imagery is loaded from OpenStreetMap’s public tile service, so when you look at the map, the area you are viewing, along with your IP address, is visible to them. When you search for a place by name, the words you type are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim search service to turn them into a location.

Neither request includes your device’s own position, your saved places, or anything tied to your account, and the reminder detection itself never uses them. It runs entirely on your device. Their handling of what they do receive is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

This website: waitlist & beta sign-ups

When you join the waitlist or ask to be notified about the Android beta, we store the email address you enter (and which button it came from) so we can email you at launch. We don’t sell it or use it for unrelated marketing.

This website: support messages

When you use the support form, we receive the topic, the message you write, and, if you provide one, your email address, so we can reply. Please don’t include sensitive personal information you don’t want us to hold.

This website: analytics & cookies

We use Google Analytics and Firebase Analytics to understand which parts of the site people use (for example, which buttons get pressed) so we can improve it. These set cookies or similar identifiers and collect standard technical data like device type, approximate region, and pages viewed. We don’t use them to identify you personally.

Notifications

In the app, notifications remind you to run a checklist. Delivery depends on your device, operating system, permissions, and battery settings.

Payments

If you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by Apple (via the App Store) or Google (via Google Play), not by us directly. We may receive plan status, subscription/product identifiers, renewal state, and entitlement information from the store. We do not receive your card details.

Data retention

Local app data stays on your device until you delete it, uninstall, or clear app data. Waitlist emails, support messages, and account data are kept while they’re useful for the purpose collected or as needed for legal, billing, security, and operational reasons.

Your choices

You can change notification and location permissions in system settings, edit or delete places and checklists in the app, and ask us to remove your waitlist email or support message via the support form. You can limit analytics through your browser settings or an ad/analytics blocker.

Children

Reembr is not intended for children under the age required to use online account services in their region.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. If a change materially affects how location-related data is handled, we’ll provide clear notice in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support form on this site or the support option in the app.
